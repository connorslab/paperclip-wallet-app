# Paperclip XBT Wallet support

Paperclip Holdings LLC provides Paperclip XBT Wallet for the Bitcoin BLAKE2b (XBT)
network. Do not send SHA-256 Bitcoin to this wallet.

## Contact us

Email [contact@paperclippool.xyz](mailto:contact@paperclippool.xyz) for wallet help,
bug reports, and privacy requests. Support does not require a donation.

Include your app version/build, iOS version, the feature involved, and a description
of what happened. Screenshots are optional; hide balances, addresses, and other
personal information you do not want to share. Public transaction IDs can still
reveal financial activity, so share them only when needed.

**Never send seed words, private keys, backup recovery keys, RPC passwords,
Lightning runes, or macaroons. We will not ask for them.**

## Common issues

- **Missing balance or USD estimate:** check your connection settings, refresh the
  wallet, and use the USD retry action if shown. A missing estimate does not change
  your coins or wallet balance.
- **Pending payment:** check Activity and payment status before attempting another
  payment. A timeout does not establish that a transaction failed.
- **Ark recovery:** a seed alone may not restore all pending operations. Keep an
  encrypted full-wallet backup and its recovery key separately. Ark funds require
  ongoing attention to expiry; notifications and iOS background execution are not
  guaranteed.
- **Hardware wallet:** verify the address and transaction on the signing device.
  Keep its seed offline; Paperclip imports public wallet information.
- **Restore or remove a wallet:** preserve and verify recovery information first.
  Removing local data does not cancel signed transactions or erase blockchain history.
